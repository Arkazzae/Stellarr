Stellarr brand guidelinesWhat it is

Talk on your own server.

What it isSelf-hosted text and voice chat for small groups who already know each other. One Docker image runs all of it.

The sceneFour or five friends, talking late in the evening from ordinary rooms. A lit desk in a dark room: the conversation is the lit part.

The characterPrecise, quiet, dependable, warm at the edges. Never playful, gamified, corporate or futuristic.

Stellarr brand guidelinesPrinciples

Principles

  • The conversation comes first.Navigation stays subordinate to what people are saying.
  • State is clear without colour.Connection, microphone and presence always carry a shape and a word.
  • Standard beats decorative.Familiar behaviour matters more than originality for its own sake.
  • It works right away.A small private instance needs none of the setup of a large platform.
  • Web and desktop are equal.Every feature has to be useful in both.

What Stellarr is not

  • Neon gaming interfaces
  • Gradient text and decorative gradients
  • Too many cards
  • Big empty dashboards
  • Animation unrelated to a change of state
  • Emoji as interface icons
Stellarr brand guidelines

Tone of voice

Stellarr talks like a capable friend who runs the server: plain, exact and unbothered.

English is the base language. Polish is a full translation written to read naturally, and its status words avoid gendered adjectives. Write in sentence case. No exclamation marks and no emoji in the interface. Stellarr is one word with a capital S.

Descriptor

Self-hosted text and voice chat for small groups.

Short description

Stellarr is self-hosted text and voice chat for small groups who already know each other. One Docker image runs all of it. Messages and files stay on your server, and calls need no third-party media service.

Words we use

Stellarr
One word, capital S, double r at the end. Never all capitals.
instance
One installation of Stellarr, as in your Stellarr instance.
server, channel, call
What people see inside an instance. A voice channel holds a call.
log in, log out
The verbs for accounts, instead of sign in and sign out.
Buttons
Name the action: Send, Join the call, Try again.
In progress
A verb and an ellipsis character: Logging in…, Opening…

Stellarr brand guidelines

Colour in use

Accent means live or actionable. Everything else stays neutral.

Accent is for

  • The primary action
  • An open connection
  • The person speaking

Accent is not for

  • Channel selection
  • Headings and decoration
  • Destructive controls, which use Danger

Text on an accent fill is black or white, whichever measures higher. With Stellarr indigo that is white.

Colour

At most three at once

SendConnectedOla

If a fourth element wants the accent, something else is wrong.

The code is MIT licensed. That covers the code and does not grant rights to the name or the mark. Draft, without legal review.

You can

  • Run Stellarr, changed or unchanged, for your own group.
  • Use the name and mark when writing about the project.
  • Say your work is based on Stellarr.

Ask first

  • Using the mark on a hosted service you charge for, or on merchandise.

Please don't

  • Publish a fork under the Stellarr name or mark.
  • Suggest the project endorses your product.
  • Alter the mark and present it as official.
